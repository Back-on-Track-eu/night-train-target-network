# About

Europe was once crossed by night trains. We believe it's time to bring them
back on track — at scale.

## The case is simple

For most trips between 500 and 3,000 kilometres, the night train is the only
realistic alternative to flying — and flying is by far the most
climate-damaging way to travel. In the EU, planes emit around 28 times more
greenhouse gases per passenger than long-distance trains
([IEA figures, via our position paper][paper]). Aviation will not decarbonise
in time. Sleeping your way across the continent already works today.

## Back-on-Track calls for doubling night train routes to 300

Europe runs about 150 today. Our [general position paper][paper] puts the
number needed at 300 — enough to triple passenger numbers within seven years
and connect every major European city. But which 300? That question deserves
more than a wish list. It deserves evidence.

## Target Network 2032

Target Network 2032 is our proposal for the night train network Europe needs.
It comes with the economics and climate effects to back every route up, built
to put in front of the people who decide European and national transport
policy. The routes run overwhelmingly on tracks that already exist. What's
needed isn't a new railway — it's the network, the right train concepts, fair
operating conditions, and the political will to fund the gap.

## The network comes from you

**You're a traveller who already knows the train you want.** Draw it on the
map, set stops and departure times, pick a train, and watch the model answer
back: what it costs, who would ride it, how many flights it replaces. Five
minutes. No expertise required.

**You're a night train expert who knows how this business really works.**
Operations, rolling stock, infrastructure, demand — a model is only as good as
what goes into it. Challenge our assumptions, bring real numbers, propose
concepts nobody has costed yet. All open source, all open to argument.

**You're a policy stakeholder who shapes where the money goes.** Every
proposal carries the figures a decision actually needs — costs, demand shifted
off planes, CO₂ avoided — route by route and network-wide. Not a wish list. A
costed one.

## Why crowdsource it?

Because the knowledge this network needs doesn't sit in one office. Travellers
know which connections Europe is missing; experts carry operational,
infrastructure and policy knowledge no consultancy could assemble alone. We
want all of it — and a proposal the community built is one the community will
defend.

## What happens to your route

Submissions are evaluated against the measures that matter in transport
policy — climate impact, demand, cost to the public. The strongest become the
backbone of the target network; we close the gaps, refine the concepts, and
publish it all as a study with a public map and method. Every contribution
credited.

The measures themselves, and every assumption behind them, are documented
across the rest of this site: start at [what this tool computes](/) or
[how to read our numbers](/reading-the-numbers).
