# Shunting and stabling calibration

This domain covers [shunting](/cost/shunting), moving the train around
stations and yards, and [overnight parking](/cost/parking), the stabling of a
train between two nights of service.

## The market top-up

Infrastructure managers publish what they charge for a shunting movement, but
that is rarely the whole cost. It excludes the staff, the locomotive time and
the yard capacity a real operator has to procure elsewhere.

The [number of shunting movements](/cost/shunting) is itself fixed at two per
trip.

## Stabling

Priced on each country's own basis against the scheduled layover and the
train's length. Several countries provide a free allowance that covers an
ordinary twelve-hour turnaround entirely, Norway and Croatia among them, so
their parking cost is zero.
Power drawn while standing is charged on top, at a rate per stabled hour.
