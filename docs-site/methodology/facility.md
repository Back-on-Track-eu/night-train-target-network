# Shunting and stabling calibration

This domain covers [shunting](/cost/shunting), moving the train around
stations and yards, and [overnight parking](/cost/parking), the stabling of a
train between two nights of service.

## Provenance

**Only 14 values are sourced.** Seventeen of twenty-eight countries fall back
on a European default rather than a national figure. Apart from station
charges, this is the weakest-sourced of the charge domains, which is worth
knowing before quoting either line.

The fourteen documents are listed under
[shunting, stabling and station charges](/sources/#shunting-stabling-and-station-charges).
The market top-up described below is not read from any of them: it comes from
Ramboll's Nachtzugstudie and an operator business case, both listed there as
cross-check sources.

## The market top-up

Infrastructure managers publish what they charge for a shunting movement, but
that is rarely the whole cost. It excludes the staff, the locomotive time and
the yard capacity a real operator has to procure elsewhere. The calibration
adds a market top-up on top of the published charge: **190 EUR** per movement
in higher-cost countries, **110 EUR** in lower-cost ones, carrying a stated
band of **±35%**.

That top-up is larger than many of the published charges it sits on top of.
The shunting line is therefore an assumption with a published charge
attached, rather than the other way round, and the ±35% band is the honest
width of it.

The [number of shunting movements](/cost/shunting) is itself fixed at two per
trip rather than derived from the shape of the service.

## Stabling

Priced on each country's own basis against the scheduled layover and the
train's length. Several countries provide a free allowance that covers an
ordinary twelve-hour turnaround entirely, Norway and Croatia among them, so
their parking cost is genuinely zero rather than missing.

Power drawn while standing is charged on top, at a rate per stabled hour. A
night train stabled all day with its systems live is not free to park even
where the track occupation is.

## What this replaced

Until this calibration, shunting was a flat 575 EUR per event everywhere. The
calibrated range is roughly 115 to 321 EUR across Europe, so most routes
became cheaper here. The flat parking rate that preceded it is now
display-only, kept for reference but not read by the cost model.
